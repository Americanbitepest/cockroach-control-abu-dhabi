Cockroaches are Abu Dhabi's most reported pest, and for good reason: warmth, food waste and plumbing give them everything they need. But not all cockroaches are the same, and treating them all the same way is why DIY sprays fail. American Bite Pest Control identifies the species first, then treats its actual harbourage.

German cockroaches — the kitchen pest

Small, light brown, and fast-breeding, German cockroaches live deep inside kitchens: behind fridges, under sinks, in cupboard hinges and inside appliances. They contaminate food and trigger allergies. Gel baiting is the professional answer — tiny bait placements exactly where they hide, which they carry back to the colony. Broadcast spraying scatters them deeper into hiding and makes baiting harder later.

American cockroaches — the drain pest

Large, reddish-brown, often seen in bathrooms, basements and around drains. They come up from sewers and manholes, especially at night. Treatment combines residual treatment of entry routes, drain and manhole treatment, and sealing gaps around pipes. If you see them regularly indoors, there's usually a breeding site nearby — garbage rooms, basements or broken drains. https://bitepestcontrol.com

Why one spray isn't enough

Cockroach egg cases survive most sprays and hatch weeks later. That's why a single visit gives a few quiet weeks followed by a comeback. Professional cockroach control schedules a follow-up timed to the hatch cycle, so the new generation is treated before it breeds. Two or three timed visits beat one heavy spray every time.

Restaurants, warehouses and apartments

Food businesses need documented, scheduled cockroach control for inspections. Warehouses need baiting along storage and loading areas. In apartment towers, the whole floor or building should be treated together — treating one flat while neighbours stay infested just pushes them back and forth.
